Avelune is a free, private and open-source tracker for your period, ovulation, fertile window and pregnancy. It is a personal open-source project, not a company: there are no subscriptions, ads, in-app purchases or premium features, and there never will be.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. Your data is stored on your phone.
• No ads, analytics or trackers.
• App lock with PIN or biometrics, and screenshot blocking.
• Optional end-to-end encrypted sync to a server you host yourself.
• Encrypted backups, and export to Excel, CSV or JSON at any time.

UNDERSTAND YOUR CYCLE
• A flower on the Today screen that changes with your cycle: rose for period days, sage for the fertile window, lavender on ovulation day.
• Forecasts shown as an honest range, with a plain explanation of how they were made.
• Calendar with logged and predicted periods, the fertile window and ovulation.
• Fits your life: irregular cycles, PCOS, perimenopause, contraception, after birth or after a loss.

LOG WHAT MATTERS
• Flow, symptoms, mood, sex, discharge, tests, pill and medication doses, water, basal temperature, weight, sleep and notes.
• Automatic ovulation detection from your temperature.
• Graphs of cycle length, temperature, weight, activity, water and sleep.
• A PDF report to share with your doctor.

PREGNANCY MODE
• Month-by-month illustrations, week-by-week notes and your due date.

HEALTH CONNECT
• Read steps, sleep, heart rate, weight, temperature and water from Health Connect (Samsung Health, Fitbit, Google and more). Health data stays on your phone.

PARTNER SHARING
• Share a view-only, end-to-end encrypted view of your cycle with one person, and stop sharing at any time.

REMINDERS
• Period, ovulation, fertile window, pill, water and more, with discreet wording.

ENGLISH AND ARABIC
• Full Arabic translation with a right-to-left layout. Follows your phone's language, or choose in Settings.

IMPORTANT
Avelune is not a medical device. Do not use it as contraception or to diagnose anything. Predictions are estimates.
