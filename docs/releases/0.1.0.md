The first downloadable release of Avelune: a free, private, open-source period, ovulation, fertile-window and pregnancy tracker. Now in **English and Arabic** (العربية) with a full right-to-left layout.

> Avelune is a free personal open-source project, not a paid product, and **not a medical device**. Do not use it as contraception or to diagnose anything. By using it you agree to the [terms of use](https://github.com/omarals95/avelune/blob/main/TERMS.md) and [privacy policy](https://github.com/omarals95/avelune/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Download

| File | For |
|---|---|
| **Avelune-0.1.0-android.apk** | Android 8.0 or later |
| **Avelune-0.1.0-ios-unsigned.ipa** | iPhone, via a sideloading tool (see below) |
| Avelune-0.1.0-ios-simulator.zip | The iOS Simulator on a Mac (for developers) |
| SHA256SUMS.txt | Checksums to verify the downloads |

### Install on Android
1. Download `Avelune-0.1.0-android.apk` on your phone.
2. Open it. If asked, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
3. Installing over an earlier test build keeps your data.

### Install on iPhone
The `.ipa` is not signed, because Avelune is not on the App Store. Install it with a tool that signs it with your own Apple ID, such as **Sideloadly** or **AltStore**. With a free Apple ID the app has to be refreshed every 7 days.

## What's inside

- **Today:** a flower that changes with your cycle (rose for period days, sage for the fertile window, lavender on ovulation day, peach on other days), a week strip, forecasts with an honest range, and daily tips.
- **Calendar:** logged and predicted periods, the fertile window and ovulation.
- **Daily log:** flow, symptoms, mood, sex, discharge, tests, pill, medication doses, water, temperature, weight, sleep and notes.
- **Insights and graphs:** cycle history, temperature with automatic ovulation detection, weight, activity, water and sleep.
- **Pregnancy mode:** month-by-month illustrations, week-by-week notes and the due date.
- **Health:** Apple Health, Health Connect (Samsung Health, Fitbit, Google) or a Fitbit account. Health data stays on your phone.
- **Partner sharing:** a view-only, end-to-end encrypted view of your cycle for one person.
- **Reminders:** period, ovulation, fertile window, pill and more, with discreet wording.
- **Import and export:** Excel (.xlsx) and CSV files, a JSON backup, and a PDF report for your doctor.
- **Privacy:** no account, ads, analytics or trackers. App lock, screenshot blocking, encrypted backups and optional end-to-end encrypted sync to your own server.
- **Languages:** English and Arabic. Choose one in Settings → Language, or let the app follow your phone's language.
